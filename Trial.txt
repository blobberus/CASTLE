OPTIONAL: THE CASTLE CHALLENGE
Two quick strategy games, about 3 minutes in all. This is optional, but top scorers get priority registration for the tournament.
You have 100 soldiers to split across 5 castles, worth 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 points. Whole numbers only, adding up to exactly 100.
GAME 1: HEAD TO HEAD
Your allocation is matched against every other applicant's. In each matchup, whoever sends more soldiers to a castle wins its points; a tie splits them. Your score is your average across all matchups.

GAME 2: FOUR-WAY BATTLE
Same castles, same 100 soldiers, but now you're in a random group of 4 applicants. Each castle goes to whoever sends the most soldiers to it (tied castles split their points). Players are ranked by total castle points, and only your finish counts:
1ST: 9 PTS / 2ND: 4 PTS / 3RD: 2 PTS / 4TH: 0 PTS
Players tied in the rankings split the payouts for those places. We reshuffle the groups thousands of times and average your results.
